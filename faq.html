01About INTEGRA

INTEGRA is a community-powered financial ecosystem where you can save, access credit, lend and invest together with other members. It also runs financial education sessions, advisory sessions and community events.

You create an account in the app, save, pick a goal or a product, and automate it. You also have access to a community, the financial workshops and the advisory sessions to keep you on track.
Members save side by side, borrow from a pool they also contribute to, and invest together in vetted deals.

03What you can do on INTEGRA

INTEGRA offers:
- Savings: Personal Savings with Dividends, Goal Savings, AutoSave and Member Fixed Savings (MFS)
- Group saving: Money Circle (Ajo/Esusu) and Shared Accounts
- Borrowing and lending: personal loans, business loans and Lend & Earn
- Learning: MoneyWise, Open House sessions, Business LaunchPad, Wealth Seminars, Financial Advisory Sessions and the #IntentionalYou series
- Refer & Earn, and INTEGRA for Groups for organisations

Personal Savings with Dividends builds your savings over time and pays dividends yearly.
Goal Savings helps you save toward a target such as rent or school fees.
AutoSave automates your contributions with tenured returns.
Member Fixed Savings (MFS) lets you set aside an amount for a chosen period with interests.

Yes. Savings with Dividends pays yearly dividends that depend on INTEGRA's annual performance, and the average over the past three years has been 22%. Goal Savings is 18% and Member Fixed Savings (MFS) is up to 22.5%, subject to product terms. Note that rates can change for members, so confirm the current rate in the app before you save.

AutoSave lets you pick an amount and a schedule and authorise INTEGRA to deduct it, so saving happens automatically.

Eligible members can, after an assessment. Approval is not automatic. For a personal loan you need an active account, completed identity verification and at least six months of consistent savings.

Personal loans for members are priced at 1.5% per month and business loans at 4% per month, subject to the loan agreement/collateral/guarantor, fees and repayment terms.

INTEGRA offers selected investment and lending opportunities as they become available, such as pooled investments in vetted businesses, sustainable ventures and financial instruments, and Lend & Earn. Each has its own terms and eligibility. INTEGRA guarantees all lending, earning and Ajo payouts.

Ajo is a group savings arrangement where members contribute on a schedule and take turns receiving the pooled amount. If 10 people each contribute ₦100,000, the member whose turn it is receives ₦1,000,000. Ajo does not pay interest.

People vetted by INTEGRA. Contributions, payout order and payment history are visible to everyone in the group.
