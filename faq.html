01About INTEGRA

INTEGRA is a community-powered financial ecosystem where you can save, access credit, lend and invest together with other members. It also runs financial education sessions, advisory sessions and community events.

You create an account in the app, save, pick a goal or a product, and automate it. You also have access to a community, the financial workshops and the advisory sessions to keep you on track.
Members save side by side, borrow from a pool they also contribute to, and invest together in vetted deals.

11Dividends

Dividends are paid from INTEGRA's annual performance and credited directly to your INTEGRA account, typically within the first 10 days of the new year. They depend on how INTEGRA performs in the previous financial calendar. The average over the past three years (2023-2026) has been 22%.

Save consistently, follow the withdrawal rules, and keep at least 25% of your total savings after any withdrawal. Completing identity verification matters too, because incomplete verification can restrict dividends.

Dividends are calculated by taking our annual profit, and dividing it among members based on their shares, savings, or business activity with the group.

12Loans

You need an active membership, completed identity verification, and at least six months of consistent savings. Approval is not automatic. INTEGRA assesses your savings history, credit score, and your ability to repay.

Depends on your Tier as an INTEGRA Member. Typically it is up to two times your total savings at the time you apply. The amount is worked out from your total savings plus your average annual savings. For example, with ₦1,000,000 saved and average monthly savings of ₦50,000, the calculated loan value is ₦1,600,000 (₦1,000,000 plus ₦600,000 a year). With average monthly savings of ₦100,000, the calculation gives ₦2,200,000, but the cap of two times your savings brings it to ₦2,000,000.

You may need a guarantor who is an INTEGRA member especially in cases where you do not have sufficient savings history. Confirm the requirement before you apply.

If you are asking for no more than your total savings, apply directly in the app by clicking Loans. If you are asking for more than your savings but within your limit, you also complete the additional loan request form at https://bit.ly/INTEGRALoans.
